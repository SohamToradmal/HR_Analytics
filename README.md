# HR_Analytics
Developed an end-to-end HR Attrition Analysis Dashboard in Power BI to track employee attrition, workforce demographics, and salary distribution.

Performed data cleaning, transformation, and modeling using Power Query and built relationships between HR datasets for accurate reporting.

Created DAX measures to calculate Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, Average Monthly Salary, and Average Years at Company.

Designed interactive visualizations to analyze attrition by Department, Job Role, Age Group, Gender, Education Field, Salary Slab, and Years at Company.

Identified high-risk areas by comparing attrition trends across job roles such as Sales Executive, Laboratory Technician, and Research Scientist.

Implemented slicers and drill-down filters to enable HR teams to perform detailed employee segmentation and make data-driven decisions.

Optimized dashboard performance and applied clean UI/UX design for better readability and stakeholder insights.

Demonstrated strong skills in Power BI, DAX, Data Visualization, HR Analytics, KPI design, and Business Intelligence reporting.
